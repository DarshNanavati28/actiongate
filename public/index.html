POLICY —
✦
Fail closed by default

Agent identity, action type, permission, impact and reversibility are checked before the gateway releases an action.

01

Exact action approval

Approval binds to a SHA-256 fingerprint of the complete proposal. A revision creates a new fingerprint and requires fresh authorization.

02

Human separation

The requester cannot approve their own action. A self-approval attempt is refused and added to the audit history.

03

Safe without AI

Unavailable or invalid advisory output falls back to explicit rules. Each decision records whether advisory or policy-only logic produced it.
